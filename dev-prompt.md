Character pose sheet for Dev, a simple cube AI agent mascot. Use one consistent character design across all poses: rounded cube body, oversized blank ski-goggle safety visor with NO visible eyes, expressive thick eyebrows above the goggles, round side hinge cylinders, segmented robot arms with pincer claw hands, chunky little boots. IMPORTANT: no mouth on any pose. Express emotion only through eyebrows, body posture, goggle tilt, and claw gestures. Style: black-and-white fat-marker sketch, thick confident strokes, clean solid outlines, very little shading, no soft pencil rendering, no hatching except minimal edge accents, white background. Create 12 poses on one clean sheet, arranged evenly with no panel borders. Poses: neutral standing, happy wave, confused shrug, focused working on laptop, frustrated at laptop, triumphant fixed-it pose, summoned/appearing pose, inspecting with magnifier, carrying toolbox, typing on laptop, pointing at a bug/error, exhausted sitting pose. No text labels, no numbers, no logos, no background.
